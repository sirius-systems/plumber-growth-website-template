## 1. Purpose

This document defines how the Plumber Growth System will be packaged, positioned, priced, sold, fulfilled, and expanded.

Its objectives are to:

- Make the offer easy for plumbing companies to understand
- Connect product features to business outcomes
- Reduce purchasing friction
- Establish firm scope boundaries
- Protect fulfillment capacity and gross margin
- Disclose usage-based expenses
- Define qualification requirements
- Prevent unsupported guarantees
- Create a logical future upgrade path

## 7. Recommended Scope Limits

The $297 price requires explicit limits.

### 7.1 Initial revisions

Recommended allowance:

- Two consolidated revision rounds before launch

A revision round is one organized list of changes submitted by the authorized client contact.

Unused revision rounds do not convert into ongoing development credits.

### 7.2 Monthly updates

Recommended allowance:

- Up to 30 minutes of routine website updates per billing month

Examples:

- Updating business hours
- Replacing an existing image
- Updating a phone number
- Correcting approved text
- Adding a short credential
- Updating financing information
- Changing an existing call to action

Unused time does not roll over.

### 7.3 Out-of-scope updates

The following require separate pricing:

- New page templates
- New functionality
- Major layout changes
- Complete page rewrites
- Extensive service additions
- Custom integrations
- Interactive calculators
- Large image-processing requests
- Branding or logo development
- Multilingual content
- Large service-area expansions
- Dispatch-system integration

### 7.4 Support boundaries

The subscription does not include unlimited:

- Consulting
- Training
- Strategy calls
- Development
- Copywriting
- Design revisions
- Technical support for unrelated systems
- Support for third-party field-service platforms

## 8. Pricing Rationale

The $297 price is designed to:

- Be understandable to an established plumbing company
- Require only a small number of recovered opportunities to demonstrate potential value
- Lower the perceived risk of adopting the system
- Create recurring revenue
- Support standardized fulfillment
- Allow the agency to gain implementation repetitions
- Reduce dependence on a small number of high-ticket accounts
- Create a natural future upgrade path

The price must not be justified with guaranteed revenue.

A permissible illustration is:

> If one recovered plumbing job produces more than $297 in gross profit, one additional job could offset the monthly subscription cost.

This is an example, not a promise.

## 9. No-Setup-Fee Strategy

The $0 setup fee reduces initial purchasing friction.

However, it also creates financial exposure because the agency performs website and automation work before recovering its implementation cost.

The strategy therefore depends on:

- A reusable website template
- Structured client configuration
- A reusable GHL snapshot
- Efficient onboarding
- Strict revision limits
- Defined page limits
- Standardized deployments
- Strong customer qualification
- Early payment collection
- Controlled cancellation terms

### Recommended protection

Consider requiring an initial minimum service period of three months or a website activation agreement.

This must be communicated clearly and reviewed before launch because a minimum term would change the current month-to-month positioning.

Until approved, the authoritative commercial model remains month-to-month.

## 10. Usage-Based Charges

The $297 subscription does not include unlimited platform usage.

Separately billed items may include:

- Phone number rental
- Inbound and outbound calls
- SMS
- MMS
- Email delivery
- Email verification
- Premium workflow actions
- Artificial intelligence usage
- Third-party applications
- Advertising spend
- Domain registration

### Recommended initial markup

- Phone and SMS usage: 20%
- Email-related usage: 20%
- Premium workflow actions: 20%
- AI usage: not enabled in the base plan
- Third-party applications: passed through or billed separately

The final markup must be validated against current GHL costs and disclosed pricing.

### Checkout disclosure

> Phone numbers, calls, text messages, email usage, premium automation actions, artificial intelligence services, domain registration, advertising spend, and third-party applications are usage-based or separately billed and are not included in the base subscription.

## 11. Customer Qualification

A prospective customer should meet minimum qualification standards before purchase.

### 11.1 Required qualifications

The business should:

- Be actively operating
- Offer legitimate plumbing services
- Have a defined service area
- Have a functioning business telephone number
- Have the ability to respond to inquiries
- Provide accurate business information
- Complete onboarding
- Provide domain access
- Authorize required integrations
- Accept usage-based charges
- Understand that results are not guaranteed

### 11.2 Preferred qualifications

The business should have:

- An established Google Business Profile
- Legitimate reviews
- Past customers
- Existing service demand
- An outdated or ineffective website
- Missed calls
- Inconsistent lead follow-up
- Sufficient job value to justify the subscription

### 11.3 Disqualification indicators

Do not automatically accept customers who:

- Have not started operating
- Cannot verify business legitimacy
- Want guaranteed leads or rankings
- Expect unlimited custom work
- Refuse onboarding requirements
- Lack capacity to respond to inquiries
- Request fabricated reviews or credentials
- Use deceptive business practices
- Require unsupported enterprise integrations
- Want to publish services or service areas they do not actually provide

The offer should reduce purchasing anxiety without making unsustainable promises.

- $0 setup fee
- Transparent $297 monthly price
- Clear demonstration before purchase
- Defined deliverables
- No long technical presentation
- Client approval before launch
- Correction of verified product defects
- Clear cancellation process

### Refund policy direction

A final refund policy must distinguish between:

- Duplicate charge
- Unauthorized charge
- Agency failure to begin fulfillment
- Verified system defect
- Client non-participation
- Completed customization work
- Usage-based charges
- Third-party expenses

Refunds should not be promised informally. The final policy must be documented before checkout launches.

## 14. Website Ownership and Cancellation

The $0 setup fee creates an ownership question that must be resolved before launch.

### Recommended model

The agency retains ownership of:

- Core template
- Design system
- Reusable components
- Automation architecture
- Snapshot
- Internal documentation
- Deployment processes
- General source code

The client retains ownership of:

- Client-provided logo
- Client-provided photography
- Verified business information
- Client-specific customer data
- Client-provided testimonials
- Original client-provided content

### Recommended cancellation treatment

Upon cancellation:

- GHL access ends according to the subscription terms
- Agency-managed workflows stop
- Usage charges remain payable
- Customer data export follows the approved policy
- Website hosting continues only through the paid service period
- The client may request a separately priced website transition package
- The reusable agency template and automation system are not transferred automatically

This policy requires legal review and explicit disclosure.

## 17. Objection Positioning

### “I already have a website.”

Response direction:

Determine whether the real problem is:

- Slow updates
- Poor lead response
- Missed calls
- No CRM connection
- Weak mobile conversion
- Poor support
- No opportunity pipeline

Do not insist on replacing an effective website without an identifiable need.

### “Can I just buy HighLevel?”

Response direction:

The customer is paying for:

- Configuration
- Integration
- Website implementation
- Workflow setup
- Ongoing management
- Support

The value is not merely software access.

### “Do you guarantee leads?”

Response direction:

No. The system improves the infrastructure for capturing and responding to opportunities. Lead volume depends on market demand, visibility, reputation, competition, advertising, service area, and other factors.

### “Why are calls and texts separate?”

Response direction:

Usage varies significantly by company. Separate usage billing prevents low-volume customers from subsidizing high-volume accounts and keeps the base price predictable.

### “Can you make any website I want?”

Response direction:

The base subscription uses an approved plumbing website system customized to the client’s brand, services, and market. Fully custom design is separately scoped.

## 19. Unit Economics Framework

The following must be measured during the pilot:

### Revenue

- Monthly subscription revenue
- Usage markup
- Add-on revenue
- One-time customization revenue

### Acquisition costs

- Advertising
- Prospect data
- Sales labor
- Sales commissions
- Appointment-setting labor
- Payment-processing fees

### Fulfillment costs

- Website customization time
- Content customization time
- GHL configuration time
- Domain setup time
- Quality assurance
- Onboarding
- Support
- Hosting
- Third-party tools

### Retention costs

- Customer success
- Website updates
- Workflow maintenance
- Support tickets
- Failed-payment recovery
- Refunds
- Chargebacks

### Primary formulas

#### Monthly recurring revenue

`Active customers × average monthly subscription revenue`

#### Gross margin

`(Revenue − direct delivery costs) ÷ revenue`

#### Customer acquisition cost

`Total sales and marketing cost ÷ new paying customers`

#### Simple customer lifetime

`1 ÷ monthly customer churn`

#### Revenue-based lifetime value

`Average monthly revenue per account × estimated customer lifetime`

#### Contribution margin

`Account revenue − account-specific acquisition, usage and fulfillment costs`

## 20. Pilot Guardrails

The first pilot should:

- Accept a limited number of plumbing companies
- Use founder-led sales
- Use founder-led onboarding
- Track implementation time
- Track support volume
- Record every scope exception
- Track form and workflow reliability
- Track customer activation
- Track early cancellation signals
- Avoid custom features
- Avoid premature add-ons
- Collect legitimate testimonials
- Update the offer using observed evidence

### Recommended pilot size

Five plumbing companies.

This is large enough to expose recurring implementation problems while remaining small enough for close founder oversight.
